Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you book a call.

What does Macaca AI do?

Macaca AI builds agentic AI for customer care, sales, compliance and core operations. Our products analyze calls and emails, understand tone, emotion and intent, and automate the work that follows.

Which systems does it connect to?

Macaca AI coordinates across systems such as ERP, CRM and ITSM without manual intervention, and works alongside the phone, email and knowledge tools you already use.

Is our data secure?

Yes. You can deploy in our SOC 2 certified multi-tenant cloud or in your own on-premise VPC with complete data sovereignty. Every interaction gets an instant audit trail.

How quickly can we go live?

It depends on your systems and the products you choose. One client scaled to 25,000+ daily call audits in under three weeks. Book a call and we will give you a realistic plan.

Which product should we start with?

Most teams start with Audit Chimp to understand and audit every call. Inbox Peel and Banana Bot are coming soon, and you can join early access now.

Do AI agents replace our team?

No. Agents handle routine work and escalate high-impact cases to your people with full context, so your team can focus on the conversations that need a human.
